Before publishing, update any remaining placeholder social links or business text
as needed.


## Email enquiry setup
The contact form is connected to FormSubmit and sends submissions to prozivosports@gmail.com.
On the first real submission, FormSubmit may send an activation/confirmation email to that mailbox.
Open that email and activate the form. Future enquiries will then be delivered to the inbox.
FormSubmit supports HTML forms without your own backend. See the official documentation for details.
